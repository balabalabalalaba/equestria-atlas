EQUESTRIA ATLAS - an explorable pixel-art world
================================================

Fly the explorer's balloon over Equestria, land wherever you like, help the places you find,
and make friends with the other balloons in the sky. Progress is saved automatically.


HOW TO START
------------
Mac (Apple Silicon: M1 or newer)
  1. Unzip, then drag "Equestria Atlas" into your Applications folder (or anywhere).
  2. The first time, RIGHT-CLICK the app and choose "Open", then "Open" again.
     (The app is not from the App Store, so a normal double-click is blocked the first time.
     If it still refuses: System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway".)

Windows (10 or 11)
  1. Unzip the whole folder (right-click > Extract All).
  2. Double-click "Equestria Atlas.exe" inside it.
  3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".


CONTROLS
--------
  Arrow keys / WASD   fly            SHIFT   go faster
  SPACE               land / do something / pick up and deliver
  ESC                 take off (in a place) / pause menu (on the map, also QUIT)
  J  journal          M  map         P  postcard (photo album)
  F  fullscreen       F12  screenshot (no pause)     H  hide / show the HUD (for clean screenshots)
Postcards (P) and screenshots (F12) are saved in Pictures\Equestria Atlas, ready to share.
A gamepad works too. If you click away to another window (or open a screenshot tool), the game freezes
right where it is, with nothing drawn over the view, and goes on when you click back into it.
A Chinese / Japanese / Korean input method stays switched off inside the game window, so WASD and
SHIFT always fly the balloon.


BALLOONS WITH PERKS
-------------------
Stamps, helping places, races, photos and friends unlock new balloons (journal J, BALLOONS page). Each
one has a perk while you wear it: a wider view through the fog, a compass to unfound places, a faster
boost, flying faster at night, a clock that runs fast for sunset and night photos, and more.


YOUR POSTCARDS
--------------
Every postcard you take with P is saved in your Pictures folder:
  Mac:      Pictures > Equestria Atlas
  Windows:  Pictures\Equestria Atlas
To look at them in the game: press J (the journal) and turn to the PHOTOS page (the little picture
tab) with LEFT / RIGHT. UP / DOWN choose a photo, SPACE shows it full screen, LEFT / RIGHT flip
through them, ESC goes back. Press J right after taking a postcard and the journal opens on it.
In the full-screen view, SPACE shows that photo's file in its folder.


SENDING FEEDBACK
----------------
Anything you liked, found confusing, ugly or broken is useful!
  - Press F12 to take a screenshot of the problem.
  - Write down the place and the clock time shown in the top-left corner.
Screenshots, saves and settings are in:
  Mac:      ~/Library/Application Support/Equestria Atlas/game_shots
            (in Finder: Go > Go to Folder..., paste the path)
  Windows:  %APPDATA%\Equestria Atlas\game_shots
            (paste into the File Explorer address bar)
Send the screenshots with your notes.

If the game ever closes by itself, please send the file crash.txt from that same folder.
If the game does not start, run it once with --selftest (Mac: Terminal, Windows: Command
Prompt) and send the file selftest.txt from the same folder:
  Mac:      "/Applications/Equestria Atlas.app/Contents/MacOS/Equestria Atlas" --selftest
  Windows:  "Equestria Atlas.exe" --selftest
